## 1. What the Extension does

The Extension helps you **bulk-delete your own content** across six platforms: **Slack**
(built in, always available) and five **optional** platforms you connect one at a time
from the extension's popup — **Reddit, X, Mastodon, Microsoft Teams, and Telegram**. For
each platform, it uses your existing, logged-in session (or, for Mastodon and Telegram, a
credential you provide directly) to act on your behalf. It runs entirely in your browser
and sends requests only to that platform's own API — there is no Erasechat-operated
backend server for any platform. See [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## 2. Permanent deletion — use at your own risk

**Deletions performed by the Extension are permanent and cannot be undone, on every
platform it supports.** You are solely responsible for what you choose to delete. Always
use the scan/preview step — and, where offered, the CSV export — to review content
before deleting; every platform's scan results can also be individually deselected
before you confirm. The Author is not responsible for any data loss resulting from your
use of the Extension.

## 3. Your responsibilities

- Use the Extension only on accounts and workspaces/instances you are authorized to use,
  on every platform you connect.
- Comply with the applicable platform's own Terms of Service and API/automation terms —
  Slack's, and, where you connect them, Reddit's, X's, Mastodon's (and your chosen
  instance's own rules), Microsoft's, and Telegram's. Deleting content may be restricted
  or logged by your organization's administrators on any platform that supports one
  (e.g. a Slack workspace or a Microsoft Teams tenant).
- Two opt-in modes can delete **other people's** content where the platform permits it:
  Slack's "All Messages" sender mode (for workspace admins/owners) and Telegram with
  "Only my messages" turned off. Use them only where you have the right to remove that
  content.
- Deleting through the Extension does not override a platform's or organization's
  retention, legal-hold, or compliance settings (common on Microsoft Teams and enterprise
  Slack); server-side copies may survive.
- You are responsible for any consequences of deleting content (including compliance,
  legal-hold, or record-keeping obligations that may apply to you or your organization),
